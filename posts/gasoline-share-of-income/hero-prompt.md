# Hero image prompts: We still measure inflation with a tape deck

Target: 1680x1080 landscape. Generate wide (16:9 or 3:2); the crop is done with
`~/.claude/d4tp-process/hero fit`. Keep the gas can and the nearest tapes inside
the middle 80% of the frame so the crop does not clip them. No text, no logos,
no brand names on the can or the cassette labels, no people.

Eric's idea: a pile of unspooled, ragged cassette tapes next to a cracked
gasoline canister that is leaking onto them.

## Prompt 1 (the main one)

A photorealistic still life on a dark concrete floor: a heap of old audio
cassette tapes, their clear plastic shells cracked and their brown magnetic
tape pulled out and tangled in loose loops across the pile, dusty and worn,
labels blank and faded. Beside them stands a dented red metal gasoline
canister with a hairline crack down one side, tipped slightly toward the
tapes, with gasoline running from the crack in a thin stream and spreading
into a wide, slick pool that soaks under the tangled tape. The spilled fuel
catches the light in faint rainbow sheens where it pools. Hard, low
side-lighting from the left, deep shadows, a cold gray-black background that
fades to darkness. Shot on a 50mm lens at a low three-quarter angle, close to
floor level, the can and the pile together filling about two thirds of the
frame with empty dark floor around them. High detail, sharp focus on the
cracked spout and the nearest coils of tape, no text, no watermark, no
people, no brand names.

## Prompt 2 (variant: brighter, more editorial)

Photorealistic overhead still life, shot straight down: dozens of cassette
tapes scattered on a pale concrete surface, several broken open with long
ribbons of brown magnetic tape unspooled into loose tangles that run across
the frame. A weathered red steel jerry can lies on its side among them, a
split seam leaking gasoline that runs between the cassettes in dark,
glistening channels and pools at the edge of the pile. Even, soft daylight
from a window, high contrast between the pale floor and the dark tape and
fuel. Muted palette: gray concrete, faded red can, brown tape, the iridescent
sheen of gasoline. Editorial photography style, 35mm look, high detail, no
text, no watermark, no people, no brand names.

## Prompt 3 (variant: tighter, single cassette)

Extreme close-up, photorealistic: one cracked audio cassette lying in a
shallow pool of spilled gasoline, its magnetic tape unspooled and floating in
the fuel, the liquid showing faint rainbow iridescence. Behind it, out of
focus, the base of a dented red gasoline can and more tangled tape receding
into shadow. Low angle, close to the surface of the liquid, shallow depth of
field, hard directional light raking from the side. Dark, moody, industrial.
High detail on the cassette's cracked shell and the wet tape, no text, no
watermark, no people, no brand names.

## Notes for generating

- Ask for 16:9 or 3:2 and generate at the largest size the tool offers; `hero
  fit` crops from the center, so leave headroom on all four sides.
- Avoid: readable words on cassette labels, brand marks on the can (Jerry cans
  often carry molded logos), lit flames or fire of any kind, and hands.
- If the tool renders the tape too neatly, ask for "tangled, knotted, chaotic
  loops of tape, some of it crushed flat."
